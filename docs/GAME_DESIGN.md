# MOKO: The Lost Hour — Game Design

## Premise
Moko wakes in a place caught between one minute and the next. A missing hour has fractured into four Memory Shards. Recovering them restores the clock and reveals why Moko entered the Lost Hour.

## Core loop
Explore compact interconnected rooms, solve environmental puzzles, avoid temporal hazards, recover Memory Shards, and reach the Clock Chamber before time expires.

## PS1 direction
Native 320x240 presentation, low-poly/limited-palette aesthetic, fixed cinematic spaces, readable HUD, D-pad movement and controller-first design.

## Planned complete structure
1. The Silent Station — tutorial and first shard.
2. The Backward Street — moving hazards and second shard.
3. The House Without Morning — puzzle rooms and third shard.
4. The Clockworks — mechanical traversal and fourth shard.
5. Clock Chamber — finale and ending determined by recovered memories.

## Systems roadmap
Player movement/collision; room transitions; interaction; shard inventory; countdown; pause; dialogue; hazards/health; puzzles/switches; save/password progression; audio; title/game-over/ending; credits.
